Added: the connection pane shows the session Panoptic has open to the server, with how long it took to connect, who it signed in as and how long the sign-in token has left, and **About this server**: product, version, edition, collation and the server's databases (account, consistency and regions for Cosmos DB). Databases that are not online, such as restoring or offline ones, are listed under **Needs attention**. Panoptic reads this once, after it first connects to the server for the explorer or a query, and again on Refresh; selecting a connection never connects to it.
